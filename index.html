03 How I Test

My QA workflow, from requirement to release.

  1. 1
    Understand

    Review requirements and business flows with BA to define what "correct" looks like.

  2. 2
    Design

    Write test scenarios and cases — positive, negative, and edge — plus the test data they need.

  3. 3
    Execute

    Run functional, integration, API, and end-to-end tests on web and mobile.

  4. 4
    Report

    Log clear, reproducible defects in Jira and follow them through to the fix.

  5. 5
    Sign-off

    Document SIT & UAT results and user manuals so the release is production-ready.
